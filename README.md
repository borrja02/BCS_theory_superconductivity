# Foundations of superconductivity. From phenomenology to BCS theory.

This repository contains a comprehensive theoretical study on the physics of superconductors, structured as an analytical progression from macroscopic phenomena to the microscopic many-body formalism. The work is largely based on the framework established in *Solid State Physics* by Grosso & Parravicini, focusing on the emergence of superconductivity as a macroscopic quantum state.

## Theoretical Scope

The study is divided into three main pillars that cover the transition from phenomenological models to the microscopic BCS (Bardeen-Cooper-Schrieffer) theory:

### 1. Phenomenological and macroscopic models.
* **London electrodynamics.** Analysis of the Meissner effect and magnetic penetration depth.
* **Ginzburg-Landau theory.** Derivation of the GL equations, the role of the order parameter, and the classification of Type-I and Type-II superconductors.
* **Macroscopic quantum effects.** Magnetic flux quantization and the Josephson effects (DC/AC tunneling).

### 2. Microscopic BCS theory.
* **The Cooper pair.** Investigation of the phonon-induced electron-electron attractive interaction.
* **Ground state formalism.** Variational determination of the BCS wavefunction and the evaluation of the energy gap ($\Delta$) and the isotope effect.
* **Quasiparticles and excited states.** Implementation of the Bogoliubov canonical transformation to resolve the excitation spectrum of the superconductor.

### 3. Thermodynamics and tunneling.
* **Thermal properties.** Evaluation of the electronic heat capacity and its exponential behavior at finite temperatures.
* **Electron tunneling.** Theoretical treatment of single-electron and Cooper pair tunneling (Josephson junctions).
